Public numbers beside ours

Does the public picture match what we measured?

We do not average scores across benchmarks: they use different scales, effort settings and model sets. Each public table is reduced to who leads whom and on how many benchmarks, then set beside who leads on our own tickets. Where they disagree, our use case is telling you something the public numbers do not. Vendor-reported tables can lean toward their author; independent ones are the better cross-check, and only the table at our run's effort is a fair match. With three models tested, agreement is encouraging but not proof.
